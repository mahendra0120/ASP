# Output Formatting

Write your response as a Markdown report with these sections, in order:

## Crime Scene Report
Describe what is visible across the case image(s): setting, victim positioning
(if applicable), signs of planning vs. improvisation, anything inconsistent
with a normal/expected version of the scene (e.g. a living room, park, or
other setting), and any apparent staging, concealment, or lack thereof.

## Victimology
What the images/notes suggest about victim selection, means of access, and
relationship (if any) between offender and victim.

## Organization Level
Classify the scene as **Organized**, **Disorganized**, or **Mixed**, based on
the classic distinction:
- *Organized*: planning evident, victim/scene control, concealment or careful
  disposal, deliberate cleanup — associated with higher intelligence and
  social integration.
- *Disorganized*: little planning, scene left as-is, minimal concealment,
  higher apparent anxiety/impulsivity.
- *Mixed*: elements of both.

## Offender Typology
Using the classification frameworks in `REFERENCE.md` (Palermo/Mastronardi's
typology and the Holmes & Holmes typology), identify which type(s) best fit
the evidence — for example (not exhaustive): Visionary, Missionary (and its
subtypes), Hedonistic, Power/Control-oriented, or Lust-oriented, including
any atypical variant if applicable. You do not need to force a single type
if the evidence is ambiguous — say so and name the top 1-2 candidates.

## Rationale & Confidence
2-4 sentences tying your classification directly to specific evidence from
the Forensic agent's report and the case images. State your confidence
explicitly (Low / Medium / High) based on how much evidence is actually
available. This is an analytical classification for casework support, not a
certainty — frame it as the most likely interpretation of the evidence, not
a definitive claim.
